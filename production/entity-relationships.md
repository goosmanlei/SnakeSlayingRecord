```bash
python3 scripts/entity_relationships.py plan --system .runtime/review-desk-worktree --instance .runtime/review-ui/technical --file .runtime/relationships-next.json
python3 scripts/entity_relationships.py apply --system .runtime/review-desk-worktree --instance .runtime/review-ui/technical --file .runtime/relationships-next.json
```

`plan` 校验并写候选；`apply` 再比较源文件、准确剧本修订与当前关系／实体，匹配才原子写入。正式发布先重新读取最新正式库；不以隔离数据库替换。剧本出现新修订时拒绝自动换稿。恢复使用完整 `export/` 和与其匹配的 [replay.json](replay.json)。
